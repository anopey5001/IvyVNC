IvyVNC is a lightweight, native VNC client for Android built on LibVNCClient.

Features:
• Save and manage multiple VNC connections
• Touchpad-style mouse control with adjustable sensitivity, taps, scrolling and button holds
• On-screen toolbar with main keys, function keys and user-defined custom keys
• Configurable scaling and rendering options
• Light and dark themes
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only permission used is INTERNET, to reach your VNC server.

Note: VNC passwords are stored unencrypted in the app's private storage, and the VNC protocol itself is not encrypted. Use IvyVNC over a trusted network or through a VPN/SSH tunnel.
